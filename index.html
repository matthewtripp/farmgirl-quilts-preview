Meet the Maker
"Life is too busy — slow down and create something just your own at Farmgirl Quilts Studio!"
— Tina Baker, Owner, Quilter & Maker

Tina studied at Northern Michigan University and Kendall College of Art and Design. She lives on 38 acres in Beaver Grove, on the beautiful shores of Lake Superior, with her family, her flock of hens and two Great Pyrenees.

You'll find her working daily in the studio — creating, designing projects and patterns, and teaching all she knows. Her motto says it all: Make it Your Own.
